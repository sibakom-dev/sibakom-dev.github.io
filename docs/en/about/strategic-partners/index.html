    Strategic Partner

    We believe that successful technology solutions are built through strong collaboration and trusted partnerships. By working closely with leading global technology providers, we are able to deliver integrated systems that combine proven technologies with local expertise and project experience.

    Our strategic partnerships strengthen our ability to provide reliable solutions across environmental observation, data infrastructure, communication networks, and decision support systems. Through this collaborative approach, Sibakom helps organizations access the technologies and capabilities they need to support their operational goals.

    The Strategic Imperative: Safeguarding Indonesia and the ASEAN Region

    SIBAKOM’s commitment to securing premier global alliances is driven by the severe environmental realities of our region:

    • The Cause: High-Volatility Geographies & The Ring of Fire Indonesia and the broader ASEAN region occupy an incredibly vast, complex archipelagic landscape wrapped in active marine territories and volatile volcanic zones. This unique geographical positioning makes the region highly prone to rapid, high-impact crises. Ranging from abrupt tropical cyclones and extreme maritime storms to sudden volcanic eruptions that eject hazardous ash into critical airspace.
    • The Effect: Why Cutting-Edge Global Alliances Are Non-Negotiable Because these rapid natural phenomena directly threaten primary economic lifelines. Such as international aviation networks, maritime trade routes, and coastal communities. Standard, off-the-shelf tracking systems are simply insufficient. To combat this, SIBAKOM actively partners with the world’s elite atmospheric and aerospace pioneers. By deploying localized, ultra-precise early warning networks, we transform unpredictable regional risks into bulletproof operational resilience, protecting commercial industries and saving civilian lives.

    Our Global Alliance Ecosystem

    We have carefully curated an elite network of partners to deliver unmatched technological superiority across the land, sea, and sky:

    1. BMKG (The Indonesian Agency for Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics)

    • Domestic Anchor for National Safety: As the primary government authority for weather, climate, and geophysical observation in Indonesia, BMKG is SIBAKOM's most critical domestic partner.
    • Proven Track Record: SIBAKOM has proudly served as the trusted system integrator for BMKG on over 100 mission-critical projects. Our collaborations span large-scale Doppler weather radar installations to fully integrated, real-time public broadcasting studios and localized maritime monitoring networks.

    2. Collecte Localisation Satellites (CLS)

    • Space-Backed Satellite Telemetry & Marine Observation: A prestigious subsidiary of the French Space Agency (CNES), CLS is the world pioneer of the legendary Argos satellite system.
    • Deep-Ocean Command: SIBAKOM partners with CLS to deploy highly robust marine drifters and advanced oceanographic buoys. Utilizing CLS’s elite satellite telemetry and cloud analytics, we provide continuous, real-time tracking of critical maritime meteorological data regardless of how harsh or remote ocean conditions become.

    3. Baron Weather

    • Advanced Weather Radar & Next-Gen Broadcast Solutions: Baron Weather is a renowned global leader in critical weather intelligence and high-fidelity data visualization.
    • Unparalleled Precision: Through our exclusive partnership, SIBAKOM deploys and integrates Baron's state-of-the-art dual-polarization Doppler Radars alongside their advanced Baron Lynx broadcasting software. Together, we empower local meteorologists with unparalleled severe weather detection, transforming complex meteorological layers into dynamic, lifelike 3D assets for the public.

    4. Raymetrics

    • 3D LIDAR Systems & Atmospheric Remote Sensing: Raymetrics specializes in developing cutting-edge atmospheric LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) technology.
    • Securing the Skies: Our strategic partnership allows SIBAKOM to integrate powerful 3D LIDAR systems for the real-time tracking of atmospheric aerosols, air pollution dispersal, and critical volcanic ash monitoring. This specialized environmental surveillance is absolutely essential for safeguarding the Indonesian and ASEAN aviation sectors from invisible airborne hazards.

    5. Sterela

    • Leading provider of automatic weather observation systems, marine meteorological solutions, and environmental monitoring technologies.
    • Extensive experience supporting national meteorological services and critical infrastructure projects worldwide.

    Complete Synergy: Technology Powered by Human Mastery

    Deploying highly advanced technology from our global partners is only the beginning. To ensure these sophisticated systems operate at peak performance, SIBAKOM pairs every deployment with our premier Capacity Building programs. We provide specialized, factory-certified training and operational integration for local teams and duty operators. By ensuring your workforce completely understands and masters these cutting-edge platforms, we transform elite global innovations into seamless, flawlessly executed local solutions.
