    Advanced Weather Radar System

    Our weather radar solutions provide the ultimate toolkit for early warning detection, quantitative precipitation estimation (QPE), and storm cell tracking across the archipelago. Engineered to withstand complex tropical environments, these systems combine SIBAKOM's local technical expertise with the industry-leading standards of Baron Weather. We deliver uncompromised data quality and real-time atmospheric visibility to safeguard national infrastructure and protect communities.

    Dual-Polarization Precision

    By transmitting both horizontal and vertical pulses, our systems accurately map the true 3D geometry of precipitation, setting the standard for tropical weather surveillance.

    • Immediate differentiation between heavy rain, destructive hail, and non-meteorological debris.
    • Enhanced quantitative precipitation estimation (QPE) to empower water management and climate-resilient agriculture.
    • Maximum early warning lead time for hydrometeorological hazards like severe flooding and landslides.

    Advanced Clutter Suppression Engine

    Navigating Indonesia's complex topography requires superior signal processing. Our systems utilize exclusive technologies like Baron ClearScan™️ and CLEAN-AP to dynamically filter out interference.

    • Automatic filtering of ground clutter from dense tropical foliage, mountainous terrain, and urban structures.
    • Real-time dynamic filtering without data loss to deliver a clear, uncompromised meteorological display.
    • Optimized storm cell tracking and boundary layer detection across challenging landscapes.

    Optimized Radar Configurations

    Engineered to meet the exact mission requirements of our tropical archipelago, SIBAKOM deploys customized radar systems tailored for specific environmental and infrastructural needs.

    • S-Band Radar: Features high-power transmitters (up to 1-megawatt) that pierce through heavy, localized downpours without signal attenuation.
    • C-Band Radar: Provides an optimal balance of infrastructural footprint and cost-efficiency for comprehensive nationwide regional tracking.
    • X-Band Radar: Delivers hyper-detailed, high-resolution imagery to fill critical blind spots in dense metropolitan areas and critical industrial zones.
