    Meteorology Broadcasting Studio (MBS)

    SIBAKOM has successfully engineered and delivered the state-of-the-art Meteorology Broadcast Studio at the Indonesian Agency for Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics (BMKG) Headquarters in Central Jakarta. This landmark project marks a revolutionary milestone in public safety communication, upgrading BMKG's previous mini studio infrastructure into a fully integrated, comprehensive public broadcasting hub. As a premier System Integrator for Environmental Solutions, SIBAKOM has seamlessly deployed an end-to-end operational ecosystem that empowers meteorologists to translate complex scientific data into real-time, dynamic, and easily understandable public weather alerts.

    Strategic Technology Benefits for Indonesia

    • Mitigating Tropical Climate Risks: As a vast tropical, archipelagic nation, Indonesia stands on the frontlines of severe weather anomalies and the escalating effects of climate change. High-impact hydrometeorological events, such as tropical cyclones, extreme precipitation, and flash floods, pose a constant threat to national stability and community safety.
    • Safeguarding Key Economic Sectors: Clear, visual, and highly reliable broadcasting ensures that early warnings are distributed massively and efficiently. This actionable intelligence allows citizens and industries to make proactive decisions, effectively protecting lives and securing Indonesia’s primary economic pillars, including maritime logistics, aviation, tourism, fishing, and agriculture.
    • Bridging the Meteorological Information Gap: Traditional weather coverage across the national media landscape has historically been brief and unsophisticated. This advanced studio establishes a dedicated vehicle for continuous, engaging nowcasting, transforming abstract meteorological data into clear visual formats that maximize public comprehension and disaster preparedness.

    The Advanced Technology Ecosystem Delivered by SIBAKOM

    The modern broadcast infrastructure is built on four core technical pillars, carefully integrated to achieve uninterrupted, high-performance execution:

    • Integrated Data Visualization Platform: The studio directly ingests massive, conventional datasets from the Tropical Cyclone Forecast and Production System (TCFPS) Data Hub. This includes weather radar tracking, real-time satellite imagery, and advanced numerical ocean-atmosphere model outputs, most notably the INA-CAWO model. Utilizing the cutting-edge Baron Lynx Broadcast software, the system unifies these diverse data layers into a single platform to render high-resolution animations in real-time.
    • Interactive Studio Environment & Hand-Tracking: To drive audience engagement, the facility introduces sophisticated Baron Hand-Tracking technology. Through optimized camera sensors, the system translates physical hand gestures into digital commands, enabling anchors to manipulate maps and interact directly with complex on-screen data fluidly during live or recorded broadcasts.
    • Augmented Reality (AR) & Virtual Sets: The studio deploys Augmented Reality (AR) technology alongside vMix software to superimpose three-dimensional digital weather assets onto the physical environment. To optimize spatial efficiency, a digital Virtual Studio system generates high-depth scenic backgrounds. This setup allows presenters to instantly switch between studio styles without the cost or storage constraints of physical props, maximizing the utility of the 65-square-meter facility.
    • Professional Production Hardware: The studio is equipped with premium, industry-standard equipment. This includes Blackmagic URSA Broadcast G2 4K cameras, Blackmagic ATEM SDI Extreme ISO switchers, Ikan teleprompters, Samsung smart preview monitors, and a specialized Godox LA150Bi lighting system mounted on flexible ceiling track rails. Audio clarity is maintained through Sennheiser wireless lavalier systems, Allen & Heath mixing consoles, and Presonus studio interfaces. System processing is anchored by high-performance Lenovo Legion T7 workstations and robust Network Attached Storage (NAS) systems.
    • Robust Operational Continuity & Climate Controls: To shield high-end electronics from Indonesia's high-humidity climate, SIBAKOM configured dedicated air circulation and dehumidification units that protect camera optics from moisture fogging. Furthermore, an APC Smart-UPS system serves as both a power stabilizer and an automated backup power source, guaranteeing 24/7 mission-ready operations and zero broadcast interruptions during building power outages.

    Human Capital and Capacity Building

    Recognizing that elite technology requires world-class skills, SIBAKOM spearheaded extensive training initiatives to ensure operational proficiency. Selected BMKG personnel participated in a rigorous Factory Training and short-term anchor course at the Baron Weather Inc. headquarters in Huntsville, Alabama, USA, gaining hands-on expertise with advanced systems. Following this, comprehensive on-site training was conducted at the BMKG HQ for 40 participants, establishing specialized workflows for duty forecasters (acting as technical super-users), studio operators, and on-camera weather anchors.

    Multiplatform Dissemination & Future Impact

    The integration of vMix production capabilities and Baron Lynx WeatherShare tools allows the production team to instantly stream live, high-definition or 4K content directly to BMKG’s official YouTube and Facebook channels. Simultaneously, the system automates the extraction of high-impact clips and images optimized for rapid distribution on social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok, ensuring critical safety information reaches the public across all modern media channels.

    SIBAKOM takes immense pride in the successful completion of the Meteorology Broadcast Studio project, which stands as a shining testament to our capabilities in environmental engineering. Moving forward, SIBAKOM remains deeply dedicated to expanding its contributions to national resilience, continuing to deploy world-class infrastructural technology that safeguards communities and builds a weather-ready Indonesia.
