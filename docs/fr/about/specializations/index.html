    Domain Expertise

    Mastering Environmental Observation

    As Indonesia's premier system integrator, SIBAKOM does not just supply equipment—we engineer end-to-end observational ecosystems. Our specialized engineering teams possess deep domain knowledge across multiple meteorological disciplines, ensuring that every sensor, server, and transmission node operates flawlessly.

    Radar Systems Engineering

    Integrating large-scale Doppler Weather Radars requires exact precision. Our engineering specialization covers the entire lifecycle of radar deployment:
      - Structural engineering for radome and tower construction.

      • Dual-polarization calibration and signal processing configuration.

      • Seamless data integration into national meteorological grids.

    Marine & Coastal Meteorology

    The Indonesian archipelago presents unique challenges for offshore observation. We specialize in ruggedized, autonomous marine deployments:
      - Deployment of Sterela Neptune AWS for harsh coastal environments.

      • Integration of CLS Argos telemetry for deep-ocean drifter buoys.

      • Mooring design and marine sensor calibration (wave height, salinity, SST).

    HPC Infrastructure for NWP

    Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP) demands massive computational power. Our IT infrastructure specialists bridge the gap between meteorology and data center architecture:
      - Design of High-Performance Computing (HPC) clusters specifically tuned for WRF modeling.

      • Implementation of N+1 redundant cooling and power systems.

      • High-bandwidth data ingestion pipelines for real-time forecasting.

    System Integrator for Environmental Solutions.

    Providing precision meteorological, maritime, and infrastructural technology across Indonesia.
