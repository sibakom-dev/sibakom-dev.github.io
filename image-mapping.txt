--------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO UPDATE THEM:
--------------------------------------------------------
Option A (Easiest): 
1. Delete the old picture inside the folder.
2. Put your new picture in the same folder.
3. Name your new picture EXACTLY the same as the old one (e.g., `ocean-buoy.jpg`). 

Option B:
1. Drop your new picture into the folder with any name (e.g., `my-new-radar.jpg`).
2. Open the corresponding `index.md` file.
3. Update the very top line to match: `heroImage: ./images/my-new-radar.jpg`
