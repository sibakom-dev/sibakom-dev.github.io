## Why flash floods demand a faster answer?

According to the World Meteorological Organization (WMO), flash floods are the deadliest form of flooding.

The hardest question in a flash flood is _where_. Traditional flood modeling can take hours to produce results, time that forecasters do not have when a warning has to go out now.

![Knowing exactly which streets and neighborhoods will flood turns a general alert into an effective warning.](/assets/images/pasted-image-1792643033854.svg "Knowing exactly which streets and neighborhoods will flood turns a general alert into an effective warning.")

## Meet Flood Finder

Flood Finder is a physics-trained AI flood model that couples weather radar with Baron's weather modeling. It is built around the natural structure of water basins and engineered for the speed of operational forecasting. Instead of only mapping flood extent, it shows what floodwater will do on the ground over time.

- **On demand:** results in as little as 5 to 10 minutes, and the model can be run as often as needed.
- **Street-level detail:** depth, velocity and direction resolved at the resolution of the available terrain data, as fine as 10 meters.
- **Impact-based:** helps forecasters identify likely impact areas and issue alerts earlier.
- **Easy to integrate:** outputs in industry-standard formats such as GeoTIFF.

![The Flood Finder interface: step through a simulation over time and view flood depth on a map.](/assets/images/pasted-image-1792643115672.svg "The Flood Finder interface: step through a simulation over time and view flood depth on a map.")

## How it works

- **Start a run.** The user initiates a flood model run for the area of interest.
- **Ingest rainfall data.** The AI model takes real-time radar precipitation estimates (QPE) and precipitation forecasts (QPF) from Baron's high-resolution 3-km weather model.
- **Get results in minutes.** Depth, velocity and direction of floodwater are delivered, depending on basin or domain size.

For the sharpest results, a local C-band or S-band weather radar provides the rainfall estimates. Radar closer to the target area improves accuracy and spatial detail.

## Built for national weather and disaster agencies

For National Meteorological and Hydrological Services, this speed makes impact-based flash flood warnings operationally achievable, even without an existing flood modeling program. Baron can supply radar, weather modeling and AI flood modeling as one complete package, or work alongside the infrastructure an agency already has. The approach supports the WMO's _Early Warnings for All_ initiative, which aims to protect everyone from hazardous weather, water and climate events through early warning systems by the end of 2027.
