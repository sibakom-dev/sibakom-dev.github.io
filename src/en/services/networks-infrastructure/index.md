<h3>Electrical & Power Resilience</h3>
Weather operations cannot pause during power grid failures. We architect robust electrical backbones that ensure continuous operation.




- **Redundant N+1 UPS Systems:** Instantaneous battery backup conditioning.

- **Industrial Generators:** Long-duration diesel or gas generators for extended outages.

- **Precision PDUs:** Intelligent power distribution units with remote monitoring and switching.

<h3>Precision Cooling & Fire Suppression</h3>
HPC clusters generating massive weather models output tremendous heat. Our thermal management solutions protect your investment.




- **In-row Cooling:** Targeted high-density cooling exactly where it's needed.

- **Aisle Containment:** Hot/cold aisle isolation for maximum thermodynamic efficiency.

- **Clean-agent Suppression:** FM-200 or NOVEC gas systems that instantly extinguish fires without leaving corrosive residue on sensitive PCBs.
