Provide the ultimate toolkit for precision weather data, micro-climate analytics, and automated monitoring across the Indonesian landscape. Engineered to transform agricultural productivity, these systems combine SIBAKOM's systems integration expertise with advanced localized sensors. We deliver real-time data and actionable alerts to optimize resource management, predict environmental risks, and safeguard farming livelihoods

**Crop Modeling & Soil Analytics**

We provide localized automated agrometeorology weather stations (AAWS) equipped with advanced sensors specifically tailored for precision agricultural optimization.

- Multi-depth soil moisture probes to monitor ground conditions and optimize irrigation schedules.
- Solar radiation sensors tracking Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) for precise crop growth modeling.
- Leaf wetness indicators to accurately predict and prevent devastating fungal disease outbreaks.

**Drought & Flood Early Warning**

Our systems provide the critical lead time and data-driven insights needed to protect vulnerable crops and regional livelihoods from extreme weather events.

- High-resolution rain gauges and radar integration for precise, real-time precipitation metrics.
- Automated SMS and dashboard alerts to notify regional governments and farming cooperatives of impending water stress or inundation risks.
